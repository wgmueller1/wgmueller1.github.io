I have a Ph.D. in Economics.  My <a href="https://repository.tamu.edu/bitstream/handle/1969.1/ETD-TAMU-2012-08-11705/MUELLER-DISSERTATION.pdf?sequence=2">Ph.D. thesis</a> examined reinforcement learning in social networks. I also have an M.S. in Applied Mathemics focusing on stochastic processes and computational finance.  I spent 2.5 years working on DARPA's XDATA and Memex projects.  I was an adjunct professor of Mathematics at Georgetown University from 2014-2017.  I worked as a machine learning consultant at FINRA before joining Leidos as Sr. Research Scientist. I am now serve as the Director of Forecasting and Anomaly Detection and Technical Fellow in the AI Accelerator.
